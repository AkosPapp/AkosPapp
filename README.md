## 🚀 About Me

I graduated from **HTL Wiener Neustadt** (2026, Informatics with a specialization in Robotics, diploma thesis graded *sehr gut*) and am now pursuing my Bachelor's in Computer Engineering at TU Wien. My focus lies on **robotics**, **artificial intelligence**, and **DevOps** — ideally at the intersection of all of them. When I'm not competing, I run a **multi-host NixOS** setup and enjoy keeping everything reproducible.

I grew up competing with the **robo4you** robotics club of my high school and am now a member of **flyby racing**, an autonomous drone racing team of TU Wien and robo4you students, working on perception, state estimation, and RL-based control.
